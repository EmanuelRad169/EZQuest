# EZQuest — Review Generation Kit (Judge.me)

**Why I'm not "seeding" reviews for you:** writing fake or AI-generated reviews violates Judge.me's terms and the FTC's rules on endorsements (as of 2024, fake reviews carry per-violation penalties), and a premium brand that gets caught loses far more trust than one showing zero reviews. So this kit is the fastest *legitimate* path to real reviews before and just after launch.

---

## The fastest legitimate sources (in priority order)

1. **Existing/past EZQuest customers.** The brand has sold since 1994 and on Amazon. Import prior buyers into Judge.me and send a review request. If you have Amazon order history or an old customer list, this is your single biggest and fastest source of genuine 4–5★ reviews.
2. **Migrate existing Amazon reviews (properly).** Judge.me has an official Amazon review importer. Because these are real, verified reviews for the *same products*, they can be displayed as long as you follow Judge.me's attribution rules (marked as imported, not presented as native store reviews). This can populate hero PDPs with dozens of authentic reviews in an afternoon.
3. **Launch beta / team-honest reviews.** Give hero products to real people (staff who genuinely use them, beta testers, friends/family who actually own the product) and ask for an honest review clearly posted under their own name. Real experience + real identity = compliant. Do **not** pad ratings or write on their behalf.
4. **Post-purchase automation.** Turn on Judge.me's automatic review-request emails so every new order becomes a review request 5–7 days after delivery. This compounds from launch day onward.

## Judge.me setup checklist (do today)

- [ ] Judge.me → **Settings → Review requests** → enable automatic emails; set the delay to ~7 days after fulfillment.
- [ ] Judge.me → **Import** → upload your past-customer CSV (name + email + product/SKU) and send a one-time request campaign.
- [ ] Judge.me → **Import → Amazon** → connect and pull existing reviews for your hero SKUs (mark as imported).
- [ ] Enable **photo/video reviews** and a small incentive (e.g., 10% off next order) — allowed as long as the incentive is not conditioned on leaving a *positive* review.
- [ ] Prioritize these hero PDPs first (highest-traffic / highest-margin): the 12-in-1 and 10-in-1 hubs, the 65W/90W/120W GaN chargers, the CFast card reader, and the USB4 cables.

## Guardrails (keep it compliant)

- Never write, edit, or ghost-write a customer's review.
- Never gate an incentive on a *positive* rating — reward the review, not the sentiment.
- Mark imported (Amazon) reviews as imported.
- Don't suppress negative reviews; respond to them publicly and helpfully — that's a trust signal in itself.
