Return and Refund Policy

EZQuest offers a straightforward, hassle-free warranty process. Warranty periods vary by product model. EZQuest warranty obligations for a product you have purchased are limited to the terms set forth in this document. EZQuest offers a one (1) to two (2) years limited warranty depending on product from the original date of purchase. 

30-Day Money Back Guarantee for Any Reason

Undamaged products can be returned for a full refund, regardless of the reason, within 30 days of purchase. This excludes corporate purchases or bulk orders, which is not be eligible for a refund.  (Note: Orders of 10 or more units of the same product are considered bulk orders.) 

Product Condition Requirements

  • Products with visible wear, damage, or signs of use, as well as free gifts, are not eligible for return.
  • Returns must include all original accessories.
  • All returned items must be in their original packaging.

Returns that do not meet the conditions above may be rejected.

 Shipping and Refund Terms

Notes:

  • Refund requests under the 30-day money back guarantee must be submitted within 30 days of opening a warranty claim. Refunds for non-quality issues cannot be processed after this period has passed.
  • Customers are encouraged to contact EZQuest Customer Support before initiating a return to confirm whether a return is the best option for their solution.

Return Process

Once the returned item arrives at EZQuest's warehouse and passes inspection, the refund process will begin.

How to Return an Item on EZQuest.com

To initiate a return, please submit a return request. You will need your order number and the email address used at the time of purchase. You can start your return here.

If you have an EZQuest account, you can manage your returns directly through your order history.

If you check out as a guest and do not have an order number, you can register for an account using the same email address used during the purchase. This will allow you to access your order history and manage returns.

Canceling an Order

You can cancel your order within one hour of placing it by yourself.

If you are unable to cancel directly, please contact us at support@EZQuest.com or initiate a return here.

Note: Orders that have already entered the shipping phase cannot be canceled.

How Long Does It Take to Receive a Refund?

Refunds are issued once the returned item arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection.

The refund will be credited to the original payment method used at checkout. Processing typically takes 3 to 5 business days.

Warranty Claims for Quality-Related Issues

All quality-related defects on items sold directly by EZQuest are covered by a comprehensive warranty, starting from the date of purchase. 

EZQuest's limited warranty applies only in the country where the product was originally purchased. The warranty becomes void if the item is taken outside the country of purchase or shipped internationally from an authorized online order.

For quality-related warranty claims, replacement items will be provided as follows:

  • A factory-refurbished product of equal value will be offered when available.
  • If no refurbished model is available, a new item will be sent.

Replacement products may differ from the original item in appearance or features. They may have a lower market value due to technological updates or changes in availability. EZQuest will determine the most comparable replacement product at its sole discretion.

Replacement product warranties will remain valid for the longer of the following two periods:

  • The original warranty period remaining for the defective item.
  • Three months from the date of replacement.

Note:

Not Covered Under Warranty

  • Normal aging or wear from regular use of products (including battery capacity reduction and changes in product color over time).
  • Products without valid proof of purchase.
  • Lost or stolen items.
  • Products that are no longer within the warranty period.
  • Non quality-related issues reported more than 30 days after purchase.
  • Free products or promotional giveaways.
  • Repairs or modifications performed by third parties.
  • Damage caused by external factors.
  • Damage resulting from misuse of the product (including but not limited to drops, exposure to extreme temperatures, liquid damage, or improper operation).
  • Products purchased from unauthorized resellers.

Unauthorized Modification & Warranty Limitation

Damage or malfunction caused by unauthorized repair, disassembly, modification, or the use of nonapproved parts is not covered by this Limited Warranty.

This Limited Warranty does not affect your statutory rights under applicable consumer protection laws.
